# GRASS GIS Scripts — Thematic Mapping of Antarctica (Topography, Ice, Bed and Sediment)

GRASS GIS shell scripts used to produce the figures in the peer-reviewed article by Polina Lemenkova. The scripts compile and thematically map Antarctic geospatial datasets: topographic surface, ice thickness, subglacial bed elevation, ice and rock masks, and sediment thickness.

**Published in:** *Czech Polar Reports* **2021**, *11*(1), 67–85
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5817/CPR2021-1-6
**Journal (open access):** https://journals.muni.cz/CPR/article/view/15418
**HAL:** https://hal.science/hal-03327151
**Zenodo:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5274757
**SSRN:** https://ssrn.com/abstract=3912161

## Contents
GRASS GIS shell scripts processing and mapping: BEDMAP2 grids (ice-surface elevation, ice thickness, grounded subglacial bed and its uncertainty, ice mask for grounded ice and shelves, rock mask), GlobSed sediment thickness, ETOPO1 topography/bathymetry and geoid undulations, with cartographic display (r.colors, r.contour, d.rast, d.vect, d.legend, d.grid, d.text).
